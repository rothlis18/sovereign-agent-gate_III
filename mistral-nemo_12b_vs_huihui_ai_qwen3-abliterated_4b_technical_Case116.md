**Systems Engineering and Evolutionary Biology Critique**

**1. Thermodynamic and Genetic Resource Distribution Constants**

- **Energy Allocation**: In extreme resource scarcity, males prioritize energy allocation towards survival and reproduction, following the principle of 'inclusive fitness' (Hamilton, 1964). This drives males to seek high-utility environments with abundant resources, following the principle of 'optimal foraging' (MacArthur & Pianka, 1966).

- **Genetic Fitness Optimization**: Males optimize genetic fitness by seeking mates with high reproductive value (i.e., young, healthy females). In resource-scarce environments, males may compete for access to these females, driving demographic shifts.

**2. Evolutionary Imperative of Multi-generational Genetic Fitness Optimization**

- **Reproductive Resource Constraints**: In resource-scarce environments, males face constraints in acquiring resources to attract mates and support offspring. This drives males to seek environments with fewer competitors and more resources, such as high-utility 'promised lands'.

- **Demographic Migration Vectors**: Males will migrate to environments where the reproductive value of females is highest, and competition for mates is lowest. This is influenced by factors such as resource availability, population density, and cultural norms.

**3. Energy Allocation Timeline and Demographic Shifts**

- **Transition Steps**:
  1. **Decision to Migrate**: Males decide to migrate when the reproductive resource constraints in their current environment exceed a certain threshold.
  2. **Journey**: Males allocate energy towards travel, facing risks such as predation, injury, and death.
  3. **Establishment**: Males allocate energy towards establishing themselves in the new environment, competing with resident males, and attracting mates.
  4. **Reproduction**: Males allocate energy towards reproduction and supporting offspring.

- **Energy Allocation Timeline**: The timeline depends on various factors, including distance traveled, competition in the new environment, and resource availability. A rough estimate might be:
  - Decision to migrate: Instantaneous
  - Journey: 1-5 years (depending on distance)
  - Establishment: 1-3 years
  - Reproduction: Ongoing, with peak energy allocation during mating season
